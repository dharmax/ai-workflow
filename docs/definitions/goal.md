# Goal

A **desired product outcome**: what success is meant to accomplish, not the code, delivery Ticket or isolated interaction used to get there.

A Goal is interpreted against the mandatory [Product charter](product.md); material conflict or drift requires a warning and developer response, not a quiet charter rewrite.

A Goal may relate to product [Features](feature.md) and [Stories](user-story.md) in the [graph](project-graph.md), but is neither a substitute for a concrete Story nor proof that its outcome has been achieved.
