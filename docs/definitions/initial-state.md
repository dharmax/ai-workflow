# Initial State

**Status:** Developer-agreed field.

The **relevant situation before the [Actor](actor.md) performs the [Action](action.md)**.

It can describe an existing, partial or otherwise **non-pristine** situation; never assume a clean installation, empty backlog or ideal starting condition unless specified. A primary [Epic](epic.md) may provide common Initial State; a [sub-US](sub-user-story.md) inherits its parent US's effective Initial State and may refine or replace the distinguishing portion.

**Open question:** whether a separate optional `Preconditions` list adds value. The existence of relevant preconditions does not, by itself, imply a new field; they may be expressed as part of Initial State. Do not introduce a mandatory field while this is undecided.

How overlapping or conflicting Epic/US text is composed is not yet formally settled. Do not invent an override rule.
