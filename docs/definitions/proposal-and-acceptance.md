# Proposal and Acceptance

A **proposal** is a candidate definition/change, whether suggested by a developer, AI or a tool. **Acceptance** is the developer's deliberate authorization of the exact reviewed product meaning, not an inference from generation, critic approval, persistence or a test passing.

A proposal should be inspectable/editable and inert until reviewed. Acceptance is scoped to the definition/revision and its relevant relationships; it is separate from execution, Ticket completion and verified behavior. Rejecting or abandoning a proposal must not silently change the canonical product intent.

For the mandatory [Product charter](product.md) and downstream Aspects, Features, Epics and USs, acceptance includes responding to material **charter conflict/drift warnings**. A grounded answer may change the descendant, change the charter through its own approval, record a reasoned exception, or leave the proposal unresolved. An AI cannot answer on the developer's behalf; unanswered warnings are not deemed harmless. Preserve the answer and decision with the affected revisions for future traceability.

Do not invent a specific UI dialog, permission scheme or new status from this definition; use the existing guarded review, authorization, graph and version contracts and implement missing behavior through separately approved work.
