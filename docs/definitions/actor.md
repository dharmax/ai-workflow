# Actor

**Status:** Developer-agreed field.

**Who or what performs the [Action](action.md)** in a [User/Actor Story](user-story.md). The Actor can be a person or a non-human actor, such as a coding agent or external consumer; it is not implicitly the developer, the assistant or a first-person “I”.

Identify the role relevant to the particular episode. Actor, Intent and Action belong to the US itself; do not infer an Actor from a convenience test fixture.
