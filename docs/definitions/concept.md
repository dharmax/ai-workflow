# Concept

An authoritative statement of **product/domain meaning** used to keep terminology and interpretation coherent.

Its accepted meaning is consistent with the mandatory [Product charter](product.md); material conflict/drift requires developer resolution, not quiet approval.

A Concept explains what something means; it is not, by itself, a Feature promise, an Actor action, a Ticket or executable proof. Refer to its canonical identity where that meaning is used rather than maintain conflicting copied definitions.
