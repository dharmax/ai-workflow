# Comments

**Status:** Developer-agreed optional field.

An **optional list of comments** associated with a User/Actor Story. An [Epic](epic.md) may also provide comments common to its USs.

The detailed semantics of Comments—including whether they affect formal acceptance—have **not** been decided. Do not silently convert an explanatory comment into a hidden requirement or discard it while resolving a Story. If the difference matters, ask the project developer to clarify whether the content belongs in [Expected Result](expected-result.md) or elsewhere.
