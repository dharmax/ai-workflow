# Product

The optional **canonical project charter** describing the intended product (purpose, actors, scope and relevant constraints). This is a product-definition entity, not the application binary or a duplicate of its Feature list.

The **Product manifest** is a view of that charter and its linked top-level [Features](feature.md), not a second authority or independently editable copy. Features remain valid and discoverable even when a charter is absent or they are not linked into its manifest.

A Goal describes an outcome; a Feature describes an enduring responsibility; a US defines a concrete episode. Do not collapse them into a single kind or infer that accepting their definitions proves implementation. See [the agreed manifest/Feature contract](../../plans/AIWF-S5-POSTCONDITION-DESIGN.md#321-product-manifest-and-feature-definitions--2026-10-17-agreement).
