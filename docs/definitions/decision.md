# Decision

A recorded, traceable **choice** about product or engineering direction, with relevant context and consequences. An AI-proposed Decision is not authoritative simply because it has been generated or persisted: acceptance belongs to the developer through the governed process.

A Decision preserves why a choice was made and can govern related artifacts via the [graph](project-graph.md). It is not the same as an [Aspect](aspect.md) (reusable governing concern) or a scoped executable [Rule](rules-and-guidelines.md), although those may be related.
