# Domains

**Status:** Developer-agreed optional field.

An **optional set of tags for organizational ordering/grouping** of User/Actor Stories. The term is plural: `Domains`, not one compulsory singular Domain.

An [Epic](epic.md) may also define domain tags common to its USs. Tags are not, by themselves, new behavioral requirements, Stories, mandatory Aspects or implementation scopes.

A controlled vocabulary and the exact mechanics of effective tag composition have **not** been decided. Do not invent either.
