# Rules and Guidelines

These terms describe **engineering governance**, not the actual list of instructions (which lives in its approved owners).

- **Rule:** a normative instruction governing engineering action in its applicable scope. AIWF has first-class Rule records with identity, text, scope, type, status, revision, provenance and a specialist lifecycle. Effective Rules must be resolved by their established scoping/approval machinery; they are not arbitrary fields added to Stories or edited through generic Product CRUD.
- **Guideline:** explanatory or advisory direction for applying good judgment. It helps interpret/review work but is **not silently promoted** into a first-class enforceable Rule or higher authority merely by appearing in prose.
- **Decision:** a particular approved choice with context/consequences (see [Decision](decision.md)); it may govern related work but is not automatically a universal Rule.
- **Aspect:** an authoritative reusable *concern/policy* applied to artifacts (see [Aspect](aspect.md)); it governs what must hold where applicable, distinct from a Rule's instruction on engineering action.

The mandatory [Product charter](product.md) governs product intent; engineering guidance cannot silently change it or dispose of a material alignment warning.

**Authority matters more than typography:** the [Prime Directive](prime-directive.md) and explicitly adopted engineering requirements retain their actual governing force even when written in a guidance document. Conversely, an AI suggestion is not binding without the appropriate approval. Do not duplicate the same policy's full text across Rules, Aspects and artifact comments.

This file defines categories only; actual current repository guidance is in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), the [design](../../plans/AIWF-DHARMAX-WAY-DESIGN.md) and AIWF's specialist Rule store.
