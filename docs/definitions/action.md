# Action

**Status:** Developer-agreed field.

**What the [Actor](actor.md) does** in the [User/Actor Story](user-story.md). It may describe several interactions **provided they form one coherent episode of use**.

Action describes the actor's use of the product, not how the engineering team implements it. Do not force one Story per click, command or internal method. See [Sub-US](sub-user-story.md) for meaningfully different episodes.
