# Flow

Two distinct uses of the word must not be confused:

- **Product Flow:** an existing optional product entity describing a significant multi-step actor journey. It may relate to Stories. Not every [US](user-story.md), or sequence of Actions within one US, requires a separate Flow.
- **Engineering execution flow:** the description of actual caller → callee, inputs/data, states, timing, failures, cancellation and other material paths through implementation. It is analysis, not automatically a new Product Flow entity.

Accepted Product Flows remain coherent with the mandatory [Product charter](product.md); material drift requires developer resolution. Use the qualified meaning when ambiguity is possible.
