# Ticket

A **bounded unit of engineering work**, grounded in actual product/Story obligations, an existing defect, or a justified technical need. It specifies the action to perform and enough priority, dependencies, targets and acceptance obligations to execute and independently verify it.

Current Tickets include **task** and **bug** kinds and have their own work lifecycle/claim and governance. A Bug is a typed Ticket describing incorrect existing behavior, not automatically a new Feature or US. Acceptance of a definition, claiming work, implementation and independently verified completion are distinct states.

An [Epic](epic.md) groups work; a [US](user-story.md) defines intended behavior. Ordinary product work derives/reconciles necessary Tickets **after** the accepted Story and its test reveal unmet obligations. Pure technical maintenance/refactoring may use a direct Ticket against existing behavior and safety requirements—no invented product hierarchy.

AIWF's high-level Ticket operations investigate, prepare where needed, resolve, test and independently verify through existing guarded owners. A Ticket in Done is not, by itself, proof that every linked Feature or Story is satisfied.
